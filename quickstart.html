SingleCase Outlook Add-in
Quick Start Guide

An Outlook add-in that files emails and attachments directly into your cases in SingleCase — without switching applications.

1
Select an email

Click on any email in Outlook. The SingleCase button in the ribbon becomes active — it's greyed out when no email is open.

2
Open the add-in

Click the SingleCase button in the ribbon. The add-in opens as a panel on the right side.

3
First launch — enter your workspace

First time only: enter your SingleCase workspace address, for example yourfirm.singlecase.app. Sign-in happens automatically via your Microsoft account.

4
Pin the panel

Click the pin icon (📌) in the panel header to keep it open while browsing emails. Without pinning, the panel closes every time you switch.

5
Select a case and save

The add-in automatically suggests the most likely case. If it doesn't fit, type the client name, case name, or case number. Select attachments and click Save.

6
After filing — go to the case and open documents

A summary appears after saving with direct links:

  1. Click the case name — goes directly to the case in SingleCase.
  2. Click the eye icon on a document — opens the document detail inside the case.
  3. Click the pencil icon — launches the document for editing in the SingleCase desktop app.
Where do filed emails appear? In SingleCase, open the case and go to the Case Mailbox tab. Attachments appear under Documents → Correspondence → Received.
